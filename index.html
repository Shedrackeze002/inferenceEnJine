How it works

Fixed-scope engagements of 4 to 8 weeks. Weekly checkpoints, and every checkpoint leaves a working increment behind.

Week 1

Audit

I map your documents, tools, and current spend, then write a one-page plan: the architecture, the hardware, the cost math, and what I need from your team. You approve the math before any build starts.

Weeks 2 to 6

Build

I build with code you can read while it is being written: pipelines, indexes, eval harnesses, deploy scripts.

  • Weekly checkpoint with a running system
  • Evals measured on your data, not toy data
  • Stop at any checkpoint, keep everything so far
Final week

Handover

I document the setup, write the runbooks, and train whoever operates it.

  • You own the code, the weights, and the keys
  • No per-seat license, no metered API dependency
  • Operating cost written down before you pay it

Proof, not claims

Three tracks of prior work. The numbers are measured and the artifacts are publicly inspectable.

Native inference engines

I build C + Metal inference engines from scratch on Apple Silicon, bypassing PyTorch entirely. On a 48 GB Mac, int8 quantization cut resident model memory from 36.4 GB to 25.9 GB, and 4-bit reached about 19 GB, moving decode speed from 23 to 37 tokens per second. Memory headroom is a deployment decision you can make with numbers.

36.4 → 25.9 GB (int8) ~19 GB (4-bit) 23 → 37 tok/s decode
